Aircraft models: b737.bin, a380.bin and b787.bin are generated by tools/make_lowpoly.py from simple
shapes (no third-party artwork, nothing to credit). The Southwest, Lufthansa and ANA colours are only
loose, simplified liveries. The earlier photographic models (Sketchfab, CC-BY-4.0) were converted with
tools/bake_plane.py and are in the git history; that script still works for any glTF.
